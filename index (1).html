FAQ

Common Questions

What is a pre-engineered building (PEB)?

A PEB is a steel building whose components are designed and fabricated in a factory to your specification, then bolted together on site. This makes construction quicker, more precise and cost-effective.

How long does a PEB project take?

Timelines depend on size and complexity, but factory fabrication and fast erection typically cut total construction time significantly versus conventional building. We give a clear schedule with every quotation.

What do you need to prepare a quotation?

Length, width, eave height, location, intended use, any crane or mezzanine requirement, and site drawings if available. A rough sketch is enough to begin.

Can an existing building be extended?

Yes. PEB structures are easy to extend, and we can also add mezzanine floors, roofing or cladding to existing sheds.

Do you do only supply, or erection as well?

Both. We can supply fabricated material only, or handle complete turnkey execution including erection.
